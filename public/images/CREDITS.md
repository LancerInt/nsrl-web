# Image credits

Everything in `nsrl/` is **commissioned NSRL artwork**, delivered in
`assets/NSRL-All-29-Image-Replacements/` with its manifest, size map and
generation prompts. Twenty-seven files, twenty-seven placements, none shared.
`src/content/imagery.ts` maps each file to its slot and is the only place a
placement changes.

Three further editorial stills live in `editorial/`, from the redesign kit —
see the note at the foot of this file.

The drop was twenty-nine. Two files left with the sections they served:
`about-leadership.webp` when the leadership section came off the About page,
and `news-research.webp` when News & Events was withdrawn. With no placement
left each would have been an unreferenced file, and a one-to-one mapping only
stays honest if it is maintained in both directions. Both originals remain in
the supplier drop if those sections ever return.

## What these images are, and are not

They are branded conceptual illustrations — staged glass, instruments and
specimens rendered in the NSRL navy and teal, several carrying the NSRL
wordmark on a plinth.

**They are not photographs.** Nothing on the site may caption one as a
photograph of NSRL's premises, staff, equipment, certificates or results,
and the alt text in `imagery.ts` is written to describe the illustration
rather than assert a room. The supplier's own note puts it the same way:

> These are branded conceptual/editorial scientific illustrations, not
> photographs proving actual NSRL buildings, equipment, staff, certificates
> or test results. Review all species and method depictions with the
> scientific owner.

That last sentence is an open action — see README.md. (The supplier's note
also covered the Leadership illustration; that section and its file are gone,
so the clause has been dropped rather than left describing nothing.)

## Still needs genuine photography

Illustration cannot do these jobs, and the set does not claim to:

- A real NSRL team photograph, should the site ever show people.
- Facility documentation: exterior or reception, the actual instrument
  benches, environmental test systems, the archive and controlled rooms,
  each with a truthful caption.
- Vivarium: approved animal-housing and welfare photography.

## Previous library — removed

Eleven free-licence stock photographs (CC0, public domain and a few
CC-BY/BY-SA) stood in until this set arrived. They were deleted from
`public/images/` once nothing referenced them; the licence record is in git
history at `4d577bc:public/images/CREDITS.md` if it is ever needed.

Two of them had already been withdrawn before this replacement, and the
reasons are worth carrying forward as a standard:

- `pipette-1.jpg` carried a visible `rawpixel` watermark across the frame.
- `documents-w3.jpg` was a handwritten ledger photographed in a museum case,
  standing in for the ten-year archive and communicating the opposite of a
  secure, validated, backed-up one.
