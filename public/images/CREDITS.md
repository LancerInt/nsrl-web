# Image credits

Every photograph in `public/images/` is used under a free licence. Files marked
**CC0** or **Public domain / PDM** require no attribution; the CC-BY / CC-BY-SA
files (marked ★) require the credit shown and are attributed here to satisfy it.

These are stand-in stock photographs of representative laboratories, not of
NSRL's own facilities. Replace them with NSRL's photography before launch — see
`src/content/imagery.ts`, where each file is referenced once.

⚠ **Two files are withdrawn and unused.** Both are kept here only so the
withdrawal is traceable. Nothing on the site references either, and nothing
should until they are replaced.

- **`pipette-1.jpg`** — carries a visible `rawpixel` watermark tiled across
  the whole frame, which no crop removes.
- **`documents-w3.jpg`** — a handwritten laboratory ledger photographed in a
  museum case, with a period magazine in shot. It was standing in for the
  ten-year archive and communicated the opposite of a secure, validated,
  RAID-backed one. No unrelated stock has been substituted; the archive
  frame was removed and is on the shot list in the README instead.
