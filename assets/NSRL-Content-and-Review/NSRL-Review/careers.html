Join us

Be part of our scientific team

As a contract research organisation working across agrochemicals, industrial chemicals and food and feed additives, we offer diverse opportunities for talented people at every stage of a scientific career.

A collaborative and diverse team fosters innovation, creativity and continuous learning. Employees have access to state-of-the-art facilities and advanced technologies from the first week, not after a probation period.

Professional development runs through training programmes, workshops and industry conferences, so that the people conducting the studies stay current with the guidelines and methods those studies are judged against.

Where the work happens

Three sites · Tamil Nadu

The facility

Salem, Tamil Nadu

A single accredited site houses everything — study direction, regulatory strategy and client services alongside the laboratories, the vivarium and the archive. Because chemistry, toxicology, ecotoxicology and entomology sit under one roof, a study moves between divisions without ever leaving our custody.

What you get out of it

Four things this laboratory can offer that a job description usually cannot.

4 propositions · read downward

  1. 01

    Facilities you can actually use

    450+ instruments across chromatography, mass spectrometry, spectroscopy and microscopy, in a 150,000 sq ft estate. Access is part of the job description.

  2. 02

    Work that leaves the building

    Reports from these divisions have been accepted by the FDA, the EMA and agencies across six countries. The studies you run become regulatory record.

  3. 03

    Continuous development

    Training programmes, workshops and industry conferences to stay current with the latest advancements in a field where the guidelines move every year.

  4. 04

    A team, not a silo

    Every division sits in one quality system, which means a chemist and a toxicologist talk about the same study. Careers here tend to widen rather than narrow.
