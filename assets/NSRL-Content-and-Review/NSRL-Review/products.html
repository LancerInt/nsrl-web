What a package is

An assembly, not an invention.

The catalogue does not change. Only the order you read it in.

The divisions that run them
  1. 01

    Nothing here is new

    Every study named in every package is already run by a named division of this laboratory, under the accreditation that division already holds. Packaging changes the order of the list, not the list.

  2. 02

    Grouped by submission, not by department

    A registration dossier does not arrive one division at a time. The groupings follow the schedule a regulatory affairs team actually works to, which is why a single package can cross ecotoxicology, chemistry and entomology.

  3. 03

    A starting point, not a fixed bill

    Scope is set by the authority reading the dossier and by the properties of the item itself — a surface-contact device should not be paying for implantation work it does not need. Every package is trimmed, and often extended, before it becomes a protocol.
