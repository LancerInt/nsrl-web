Controlled environments

Five conditions, each held to a stated specification.

Studies here run for days or for years. The specification column is the part that has to survive the whole duration — and be demonstrable afterwards.

Controlled environments — specification schedule
RefEnvironmentSpecificationWhat is held, and for what
01Animal housing70+ clean roomsControlled temperature, humidity, lighting and ventilation, with continuous health and body-weight monitoring, supporting the small animal models the toxicology programme runs on.
02Inhalation suitesMultiple chambersState-of-the-art inhalation chambers for acute and repeat-dose inhalation toxicity in small animals — the capability that decides whether an airborne chemical or inhaled drug can be evaluated at all.
03Aquatic & terrestrial systemsFlow-throughFlow-through aquatic systems, plant growth chambers, cold rooms and micro-applicators supporting twenty-six species across the ecotoxicology programme.
04Insect rearingIn-house coloniesBreeding programmes maintained for every entomology test species, from cockroaches and mosquitoes to termites and stored-product insects, removing the variability of field-collected populations.
05Archive10-year retentionSecure storage for raw data, blocks, slides and specimens, with RAID-server redundancy held both on-site and off-site.
