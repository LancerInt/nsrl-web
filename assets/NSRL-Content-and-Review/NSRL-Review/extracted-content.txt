Quality system
Written down, audited, and kept.
Five commitments and three mechanisms. Nothing here is aspirational — each line is something an auditor can ask us to evidence on the day they walk in. Certificates on file
Part A — Commitments
05 clauses A. 1
Ethics compliance
Every study is designed and conducted within the ethical framework that governs it, reviewed before work begins rather than defended afterwards. A. 2
Animal welfare
The animal care and use programme runs to a written standard with in-house veterinarians, continuous health monitoring and environmental control treated as study parameters. A. 3
Employee safety
Handling protocols, containment and monitoring protect the people who work with hazardous test items, radioactive material and biological agents. A. 4
Data security
Redundancy and resilience with multiple backups stored on RAID servers, both on-site and off-site, with archival capacity extending ten years. A. 5
Timely completion
Schedule is treated as a quality attribute. A study delivered late has failed the sponsor as surely as one delivered wrong.
Part B — Mechanisms
03 systems B. 1
Test item management TSC / TICO
Separate storage zones are maintained for pharmaceutical and non-pharmaceutical products. Sponsors receive receipt confirmation within 48 hours, with stability guidance issued alongside. B. 2
Data management Redundancy & resilience
Multiple backups are stored on RAID servers held both on-site and off-site, with archival capacity extending ten years for raw data, specimens and reports. B. 3
Quality assurance Independent audit
The facility maintains GLP standards and NABL ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation, ensuring the resulting data is accepted by regulators globally.
Animal welfare
Three Rs. And a fourth.
Welfare is treated as a data-quality question, not a compliance overlay. A stressed animal is a poor model — the programme exists because the science depends on it. Inside the vivarium
01
Replacement
In vitro and in silico methods substituted for animal work wherever the endpoint permits — including SAR, QSAR and cell-based assays that answer the question earlier and at lower cost.
02
Refinement
Improved procedures that minimise harm and distress, from housing and enrichment through to analgesia, handling and humane endpoints.
03
Reduction
Animal numbers optimised against statistical validity, so that no study uses more animals than the question requires — or fewer than it can answer with.
04
Rehabilitation
Post-study rehabilitation protocols, extending our obligation beyond the point at which the data has been collected.

01
Nothing here is new
Every study named in every package is already run by a named division of this laboratory, under the accreditation that division already holds. Packaging changes the order of the list, not the list.
02
Grouped by submission, not by department
A registration dossier does not arrive one division at a time. The groupings follow the schedule a regulatory affairs team actually works to, which is why a single package can cross ecotoxicology, chemistry and entomology.
03
A starting point, not a fixed bill
Scope is set by the authority reading the dossier and by the properties of the item itself — a surface-contact device should not be paying for implantation work it does not need. Every package is trimmed, and often extended, before it becomes a protocol.

The working method
What happens between your first call and your report.
We start from the authority that will read the dossier, not from a study list. Divisions run in parallel wherever the science allows, and the record stays unbroken from receipt through to the tenth year of archival. How the quality system governs it
01
Scope
We start from the authority that will read the dossier, not from the study list. The endpoints, guidelines and species follow from that decision.
Regulatory strategy · guideline mapping · study design
02
Receipt
Test items enter through a controlled Test Item Control Office with separate storage zones for pharmaceutical and non-pharmaceutical products. Sponsors receive confirmation within 48 hours.
TSC / TICO · segregated storage · 48-hour receipt
03
Characterise
Identity, purity, concentration and stability are established in chemistry before a dose is prepared. Every number generated downstream depends on this step being right.
Identity · purity · stability guidance
04
Conduct
Studies run in accredited facilities with continuous in-life monitoring and veterinary oversight. Divisions run in parallel where the science allows, which is where the timeline is actually won.
GLP conduct · in-life monitoring · parallel divisions
05
Audit
An independent Quality Assurance unit audits the study against the protocol and the GLP principles. QA reports to management, not to the study director.
Independent QA · protocol compliance · phase inspection
06
Report
Data are compiled into a study report written for the submitting authority, with the raw data traceable from every conclusion back to the instrument that produced it.
Traceable reporting · regulatory format
07

What the dossier has to prove.
An agrochemical dossier is one of the hardest submissions in regulatory science: it has to satisfy authorities in several countries at once, each with a different view of which endpoints matter. We build the package as a single body of evidence rather than a stack of unrelated studies.
Reports from this programme have been accepted by regulatory agencies in the USA, UK, Japan, China, Brazil and India, and the laboratory is recognised by the Central Insecticides Board and Registration Committee.
The division's registration history includes India's first nano-fertiliser — a submission with no domestic precedent, assembled from the ground up.

What the dossier has to prove.
The food and feed additives division evaluates preservatives, flavour enhancers, antioxidants, emulsifiers and nutritional supplements through combined safety and efficacy assessment.
Analysis covers ingredient identity, contaminant identification, nutritional content and stability, alongside toxicological work spanning acute and chronic toxicity and allergenicity.
Formulation support addresses ingredient compatibility, formulation stability and dosage optimisation, and gamma radiation detection instrumentation measures radiation levels in food and feed additives directly. Scientists and regulatory specialists track current industry regulation, working to GLP and international standards.

What the dossier has to prove.
Chemical compliance regimes are proliferating faster than most manufacturers can track. We generate the data behind REACH, RoHS, CLP, GHS and California Proposition 65 declarations, so a single substance can be placed on several markets from one evidence base.
The programme covers toxicological studies, physicochemical property assessment, environmental fate, and specialist testing including Chromium VI and product packaging.
Gas and liquid chromatography, mass spectrometry and elemental analysis support the work, conducted under GLP and international guidelines for clients in pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, cosmetics, household products and industrial manufacturing.

Optimizing quality in the clinical laboratory: focus on preclinical drug discovery
Preclinical drug discovery is the phase that decides which compounds are worth a clinical trial. The accuracy of the data behind that decision is not a quality-department concern — it is the whole argument.

NSRL

In the field of preclinical drug discovery, the importance of optimizing quality in the clinical laboratory cannot be overstated.
Preclinical drug discovery is a pivotal phase that precedes clinical trials. It involves comprehensive research to evaluate the safety, efficacy, and pharmacokinetic properties of potential drug candidates.
The accuracy and reliability of preclinical data are vital to make informed decisions about which compounds to progress to the clinical testing phase.
Our laboratory brings together a team of highly skilled and experienced scientists who are experts in various disciplines, including pharmacology, toxicology, genetics, and more. The laboratories are equipped with state-of-the-art infrastructure and cutting-edge technologies.
Preclinical drug discovery services encompass a wide range of offerings to address diverse research needs — from target validation and high-throughput screening through in vivo efficacy studies, ADME-Tox assessments and safety evaluations.
The laboratory upholds the highest standards of animal welfare in its preclinical research studies, and maintains GLP compliance and ethical animal treatment standards throughout.
