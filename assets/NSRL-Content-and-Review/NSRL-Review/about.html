Contract research organisation

One custodian, from receipt to archive.

A one-stop, fully equipped and accredited laboratory built to meet agrochemical, industrial chemical and food and feed additive regulatory needs — without a single study leaving our custody.

Seven scientific divisions share one quality system, one archive and one chain of custody. A substance that arrives for chemistry can move to toxicology, ecotoxicology and entomology without a transfer, a re-validation, or a gap in the record.

Notes

Quality system

Written down, audited, and kept.

Five commitments and three mechanisms. Nothing here is aspirational — each line is something an auditor can ask us to evidence on the day they walk in.

Certificates on file

Part A — Commitments

05 clauses

A.1
Ethics compliance
Every study is designed and conducted within the ethical framework that governs it, reviewed before work begins rather than defended afterwards.
A.2
Animal welfare
The animal care and use programme runs to a written standard with in-house veterinarians, continuous health monitoring and environmental control treated as study parameters.
A.3
Employee safety
Handling protocols, containment and monitoring protect the people who work with hazardous test items, radioactive material and biological agents.
A.4
Data security
Redundancy and resilience with multiple backups stored on RAID servers, both on-site and off-site, with archival capacity extending ten years.
A.5
Timely completion
Schedule is treated as a quality attribute. A study delivered late has failed the sponsor as surely as one delivered wrong.

Part B — Mechanisms

03 systems

B.1
Test item managementTSC / TICO
Separate storage zones are maintained for pharmaceutical and non-pharmaceutical products. Sponsors receive receipt confirmation within 48 hours, with stability guidance issued alongside.
B.2
Data managementRedundancy & resilience
Multiple backups are stored on RAID servers held both on-site and off-site, with archival capacity extending ten years for raw data, specimens and reports.
B.3
Quality assuranceIndependent audit
The facility maintains GLP standards and NABL ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation, ensuring the resulting data is accepted by regulators globally.

Animal welfare

Three Rs. And a fourth.

Welfare is treated as a data-quality question, not a compliance overlay. A stressed animal is a poor model — the programme exists because the science depends on it.

Inside the vivarium
  1. 01

    Replacement

    In vitro and in silico methods substituted for animal work wherever the endpoint permits — including SAR, QSAR and cell-based assays that answer the question earlier and at lower cost.

  2. 02

    Refinement

    Improved procedures that minimise harm and distress, from housing and enrichment through to analgesia, handling and humane endpoints.

  3. 03

    Reduction

    Animal numbers optimised against statistical validity, so that no study uses more animals than the question requires — or fewer than it can answer with.

  4. 04

    Rehabilitation

    Post-study rehabilitation protocols, extending our obligation beyond the point at which the data has been collected.
