How a programme is assembled

Why one laboratory

One laboratory, seven divisions.

Most preclinical programmes are assembled from three or four vendors, and every boundary between them re-ships material, re-validates methods and loses weeks to email.

Here the divisions share one campus, one quality system, one archive and one chain of custody. Nothing is handed over, because nothing leaves.

One test item · one custody record

  • ChemistryIdentity, purity and stability of the test item
  • ToxicologyIn-life conduct and dosing
  • EcotoxicologyEffect on aquatic and terrestrial species
  • VivariumHousing, environmental control and health monitoring
  • Quality assuranceIndependent audit against the protocol

An illustration, not a pipeline. Which divisions a programme engages — and in what order — follows from the dossier it has to satisfy, and they run in parallel wherever the science allows.
