COMMON STYLE
Premium scientific editorial photography or clearly labelled editorial illustration. Warm ivory matte surface, natural daylight, precise ordinary materials, realistic scale, generous breathing room, navy and muted teal accents. No baked-in text/logo; HTML carries labels. No holographic organs, neon, glowing chrome, floating stages, impossible machines or unknown scientific data. Do not imply generated interiors are NSRL premises.
