NSRL / DESIGN AND INTERACTION AUDIT / 22 OCTOBER 2026

A stronger visual system for scientific credibility.

The functional structure is improving, but the recurring glossy artwork, decorative motifs and unbalanced hero proportions limit the site’s appeal. Replacing more stock-like images with more fantasy equipment will not solve the problem. The kit proposes warmer editorial imagery, a compact hierarchy and meaningful interaction.

Scope: live desktop review of 19 routes including the homepage, five supplied screenshots, image-slot inspection, search tests, menu Escape, one section anchor and blank-form validation. This is a broad design/interaction audit, not a complete mobile, security, scientific, certificate or performance certification. No messages were sent.

Visual direction

Warm white background, navy typography, restrained teal, one clear focal image per section. Use contemporary editorial science photography and exact native SVG for interfaces. Avoid fabricated graphs, atom wallpaper, glowing internal instruments and anatomy holograms. Keep real-facility evidence separate from labelled conceptual art.

Delivered assets

  • Three new PNG illustrations: homepage hero, Toxicology and Ecotoxicology. These illustrate research themes; they are not photographs of NSRL premises.
  • Seven original SVG icons matching the seven public divisions.
  • A seven-division shared-quality network SVG and a reduced-motion-aware analytical trace SVG.
  • Three static SVG background motifs. Use at most one per page and keep them secondary.
  • A React/TypeScript interactive division component, self-contained animation demo, complete design prompt, asset mapping and generation briefs.

Usage

Open index.html for the visual direction and assets, and animation-demo.html for a working interaction. Start implementation with IMPLEMENTATION_PROMPT.txt. Scientific-owner review

The site retains superlative claims, regulatory acceptance statements, laboratory statistics and protocol descriptions. These were recorded, not independently verified. In particular, use owner evidence for “India’s largest,” FDA/EMA acceptance, equipment inventory, room counts and accreditation scope. This audit does not assess technical capability.
